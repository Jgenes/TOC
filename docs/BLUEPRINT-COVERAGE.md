## How to read this report

- **I** — Implemented locally with cited source/checks; not a production or business approval.
- **P** — Partial or deliberate implementation alternative; remaining work is explicit.
- **C** — Code exists but a real dependency/configuration and acceptance test are still required.
- **O** — Management/legal/content/independent real-world acceptance required; not established by local code/tests.

Explicit-ID status counts: **119 I · 28 P · 11 C · 23 O**. These are review categories, **not** a compliance percentage or a count of certified features. An I row still depends on the global content, legal, security and hosting launch gates.

Evidence links point to the implementation or repeatable checks, not real customer/equipment evidence. Synthetic fixtures, credentials, the source PDF, generated HTML and local browser reports are deliberately excluded from Git. Current executed results and limitations are in [QA-RESULTS.md](../QA-RESULTS.md). The machine-readable reviewed register is [blueprint-coverage.json](blueprint-coverage.json); regenerate this document with `npm run docs:coverage`.

## Open implementation/acceptance items — do not silently treat these as complete

### G01 — Business, branding and evidence

**TOC approval.** Official identity/contact/logo/palette, service capability/staffing/equipment/safety, licensed media, genuine projects/clients/testimonials and approved tax/pricing/contract policies must be supplied. Illustrative assets and QA records prove nothing.

### G02 — Live host and integrations

**Configuration + operational validation.** SMTP password/real mailbox receipt, supervised jobs, reviewed executable scanner, optional AI provider/processor/budget, HTTPS/secrets/persistent storage, alert destination/offsite backup/restore remain unconfigured or unverified.

### G03 — Payments/accounting/third-party messaging

**Not implemented/connected.** No payment-provider checkout/webhook/signature/reconciliation/refund or invoice/collections service, SMS/WhatsApp sending, external traffic analytics or HR integration. These require an approved provider/data/consent/workflow decision and additional implementation—not just a secret.

### G04 — Single-node infrastructure alternative

**Architecture decision required.** SQLite/private filesystem/custom CMS are implemented, not managed PostgreSQL/object storage or a distributed queue/rate limiter. Multi-instance/serverless scaling needs adapter/migration/lease/authorization/recovery work.

### G05 — Localization/SEO completeness

**Partial implementation.** Core content/views are bilingual; locale-specific routes/hreflang/full translated metadata/technical errors/diagnostics and fluent human acceptance are unfinished. Original customer/evidence text must remain untouched.

### G06 — Intake exact parity

**Partial implementation.** Primary five-division intake plus Event→Catering is detailed. Every additional division does not get an independent full field set; initial unknown dates are allowed; files use protected post-inquiry upload. Menu/package selection is not a full order/inventory system.

### G07 — Advanced operational customization

**Partial implementation.** Checklists/recurrence/ordered evidence are implemented; arbitrary checklist/SLA/calendar/rescheduling/customer milestone-signature/reallocation UX is not. Resources hold the full contract horizon conservatively. Written authorizations and acceptance remain human evidence.

### G08 — Organization and historical/marketing CRM

**Not implemented/partial.** Organization is text, not shared membership/access. No attribution visitor pipeline, external-mailbox reply import, enterprise multi-task/calendar CRM, invoice/payment or complete historical-assessment reporting.

### G09 — Full production AI/evaluation

**Configuration + additional validation.** Gateway covers eight capabilities through nine IDs with safe fallback, but no live provider, automated factual verifier, calibrated confidence, real token/currency cost meter or completed live bilingual/adversarial evaluation. Human factual/commercial authority is mandatory.

### G10 — Privacy/retention/immutable audit

**Legal/operational + partial implementation.** Manual rights review/export and draft expiry exist; general legal retention/erasure across records/mail/processors/backups, legal holds and immutable offsite audit storage require approved policy/process/additional work.

### G11 — Performance/accessibility/security/operations acceptance

**Not certified.** Local tests are not manual screen-reader/WCAG 2.2 AA/OWASP penetration testing, final-device low-bandwidth/p75 RUM target evidence, live alert delivery, independent concurrency/load acceptance, training or production RPO/RTO rehearsal.

### G12 — Design/management handover

**Partially delivered.** Code components/tokens/specifications/standalone HTML/docs are supplied. Official brand package, separate Figma/Adobe source files and signed management/usability/legal handover are not supplied.
